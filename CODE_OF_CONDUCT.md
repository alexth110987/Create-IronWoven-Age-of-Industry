Create IronWoven is a community project. Everyone taking part in its discussions, issues, and testing is expected to help keep the community welcoming and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and helpful.
- Explain disagreements without personal attacks.
- Give useful feedback and allow others room to learn.
- Respect the work and licences of mod authors and contributors.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and the issue being discussed.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Doxxing or sharing another person's private information.
- Spam, impersonation, or deliberately misleading reports.
- Publishing passwords, access tokens, or private server details.

## Reporting a concern

Do not start a public argument about misconduct. Contact the project maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/alexth110987) with a link and a short description. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly as possible.
