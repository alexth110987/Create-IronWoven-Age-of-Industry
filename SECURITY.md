Do not post passwords, access tokens, private server addresses, personal information, or exploitable security details in public issues or discussions.

For ordinary gameplay problems, use the public issue forms.

For a security-sensitive concern, do not publish the details in a public issue. Contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/alexth110987). Share only the information needed to understand and reproduce the concern.
